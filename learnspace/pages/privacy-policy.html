Family Privacy & Data Protection Policy

LearnSpace is committed to safeguarding homeschool family data, protecting minor student privacy, and upholding non-commercial community governance.

1. Introduction

LearnSpace Homeschool Co-Op ("LearnSpace", "we", "our", or "the cooperative") operates as a parent-directed non-profit educational cooperative. We respect the delicate privacy of parent educators and their children. This Privacy Policy details how we handle family information across our website, lending library catalog, and parent dashboard.

2. Information Collection

We collect only the essential information necessary to facilitate safe campus participation and library borrowing:

  • Parent Information: Parent full name, verified email address, phone number, and physical mailing address for lending library correspondence.
  • Student Information: Child's first name, age or grade level, elective subject interests, and emergency medical contact details. We do NOT collect social security numbers, birth certificates, or psychological evaluations.
  • Academic & Enrollment Records: Class attendance rosters, science laboratory completion logs, and lending library checkout history.

3. Data Usage

All collected information is strictly utilized to deliver cooperative services: verifying active class rosters, issuing campus emergency safety notices, tracking borrowed curriculum kits, generating end-of-year high school lab completion transcripts, and distributing co-op park day reminders. We never use student or family data for algorithmic advertising or behavioral tracking.

4. Account Security

Your account credentials and student records are protected with industry-standard encryption protocols. Dashboard sessions require secure authentication, and access to campus rosters is strictly restricted to verified parent members and instructors.

5. Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosure

LearnSpace does NOT sell, rent, monetize, or trade parent or student data with commercial data brokers, educational marketers, or corporate advertisers. Information is only shared internally with instructors who teach your enrolled children, or where legally mandated by safety authorities during life-threatening medical emergencies.

6. User Rights & Data Management

Parents maintain 100% ownership over their family data. Through the Parent Dashboard account settings, you may review, correct, download your child's laboratory transcripts, or request complete account anonymization upon leaving the cooperative.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

LearnSpace complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not permit children under 13 to create independent online accounts or publish public profiles without verifiable parental supervision and explicit consent. All student enrollment, messaging, and records are managed directly by parent guardians.

If you have questions regarding our privacy practices or wish to submit a data inquiry, please contact our Board Privacy Officer at privacy@learnspace.org.

LearnSpace is a collaborative homeschool cooperative and resource center empowering parent educators.
