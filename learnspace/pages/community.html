Parent Leadership

Join a Co-Op Working Committee

Our cooperative structure relies on the unique talents of every parent. Whether you have experience in science lab management, theatrical set design, outdoor trail safety, library archiving, or event planning, there is a meaningful place for you to contribute.

  • Academic Curriculum Committee: Vetting textbooks, science experiment safety, and lab syllabi.
  • Hospitality & Social Committee: Organizing park picnics, family feasts, and welcoming new families.
  • Makerspace Maintenance Team: Overseeing 3D printers, woodworking tools, and electronics benches.
  • Field Trip Coordinators: Securing educational group discounts and private venue access.

Frequently Asked Questions & Policies

Everything you need to know about our cooperative model, parent volunteer duties, class fees, safety policies, and state homeschool compliance.

LearnSpace is an educational cooperative, not a private school. Parents remain the legal educators, selecting their home curriculum and issuing their own transcripts. We provide specialized enrichment labs, lending resources, and social connection that families share together.

For Full Co-Op members, one parent from each family commits to approximately 2 to 3 hours per month assisting with class setups, library duties, event organizing, or campus hospitality. Buyout options are available for families with newborn infants or demanding work schedules.

Families pay a monthly membership fee ($45 for Resource Pass or $120 for Full Co-Op). Individual specialized classes have nominal semester lab/materials fees (typically $200–$320 per 14-week term) that directly cover instructor supplies, science glassware, and workbook consumables.

Yes. Child safety is our paramount priority. All participating parents, instructors, and volunteers undergo comprehensive nationwide criminal background checks and complete basic pediatric CPR orientation before working with children.
