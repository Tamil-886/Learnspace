Cultivating Wonder, Academic Rigor & Shared Community

Founded in 2018 by six homeschool families seeking cooperative science labs, LearnSpace now serves over 150 families across the regional homeschool community.

Our Founding Vision

A Co-Op Built on Collaboration, Not Commercialization

At LearnSpace, we believe that children thrive when their home education is enriched by high-caliber laboratory science, Socratic literature circles, performing arts ensembles, and passionate peer friendships.

We operate as a 501(c)(3) non-profit cooperative governed entirely by an elected parent board. Every dollar invested goes directly into wet lab facilities, robotics microcontrollers, fine arts materials, and our expansive lending library.

Guiding Principles

The LearnSpace Family Values

Parent Empowerment

Parents remain the primary educators and legal directors of their children's homeschool transcripts. We provide specialized enrichment to complement your home vision.

Inquiry-Driven Academics

We champion deep conceptual mastery over surface memorization through controlled experiments, primary source reading, and creative maker iterations.

Inclusive Fellowship

Our co-op embraces families following diverse pedagogical methods—Charlotte Mason, Classical, STEM-focused, and eclectic home learners alike.

Educational Approach

Our Learning Philosophy

We believe education is an atmosphere, a discipline, and a life. Children are born persons with natural curiosity; our role as collaborative parent educators is not to fill an empty bucket, but to light a self-sustaining flame.

By combining living books, primary scientific laboratory inquiry, multisensory mathematics, and Charlotte Mason nature journaling, we cultivate critical thinkers who love learning for its own sake rather than standardized test scores.
