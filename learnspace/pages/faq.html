Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our cooperative model, parent duties, class fees, safety policies, and state homeschool compliance.

LearnSpace is an educational cooperative, not a school. Parents remain the legal educators, selecting their home curriculum and issuing their own transcripts. We provide specialized enrichment labs, lending resources, and social connection that families share together.

For Full Co-Op members, one parent from each family commits to approximately 2 to 3 hours per month assisting with class setups, library duties, event organizing, or campus safety. Buyout options are available for families with newborn infants or demanding work schedules.

Families pay a monthly membership fee ($45 for Resource Pass or $120 for Full Co-Op). Individual specialized classes have nominal semester lab/materials fees (typically $200–$320 per 14-week term) that directly cover instructor supplies, science glassware, and workbook consumables.
