Cooperative Membership Agreement & Terms

Please review our cooperative community guidelines, borrowing rules, parent volunteer duties, and enrollment policies.

1. Account Responsibilities

By registering for a LearnSpace account, parent guardians confirm that all student registration details and medical emergency contacts provided are truthful and current. Parents are responsible for maintaining confidentiality of login credentials and supervising their student's access to online community forums.

2. Classes and Enrollment

Class enrollments are allocated on a first-come, first-served basis according to member tier priority windows. To ensure high educational quality and lab safety compliance, classes maintain strict maximum student caps (typically 12–16 students). Parents may withdraw or switch class cohorts up to 7 days before term commencement for a complete refund of specific materials fees.

3. Membership Terms & Dues

LearnSpace operates as a non-profit cooperative. Monthly dues fund facility rent, utility costs, laboratory consumables, and library acquisitions. Memberships renew automatically each month and may be paused or cancelled at any time through the Parent Dashboard with 7 days' advance notice before the upcoming billing cycle.

4. Resource Usage & Lending Library Rules

Our 2,400+ item curriculum library is a shared family treasure. All borrowing members agree to:

  • Treat all living books, microscopes, manipulatives, and robotics kits with gentleness and care.
  • Return loaned items within the 3-week borrowing window or request an online extension via the dashboard.
  • Notify the library coordinator immediately of any missing parts or accidental damage so replacement parts can be sourced.

5. Community Guidelines & Campus Conduct

LearnSpace brings together diverse homeschooling families from many educational methodologies. All members agree to foster a culture of mutual respect, kindness, academic curiosity, and courteous conflict resolution on campus and within digital parent circles.

6. Parent Volunteer Duties & Supervision

Cooperative members enrolled in campus classes fulfill rotating cooperative responsibilities (approximately 2 hours per week) assisting teachers, supervising playground hours, or coordinating events. Parents maintain primary legal educational custody of their children in accordance with state homeschool statutes.

7. Contact Information

For governance questions, board meeting schedules, or clarification regarding these terms, please contact our Board Secretary at board@learnspace.org or visit our campus administrative office.

LearnSpace is a collaborative homeschool cooperative and resource center empowering parent educators.
