Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to the most common questions from prospective and incoming homeschool families.

Yes, absolutely! We warmly encourage bringing your learners. Children love seeing live chemistry experiments, visiting the youth maker corner, browsing the living book library, and testing our outdoor nature trails. Please keep young children supervised during class transitions.

Guided tours typically take 45 minutes, followed by a 15-minute informal Q&A session with a parent ambassador. You'll see our 8 classrooms, 2 certified STEM labs, the curriculum lending center, outdoor pavilions, and the parent working lounge.

Our parent admissions volunteers review incoming inquiries daily and respond within 24 business hours (Monday through Friday). If you need immediate assistance during co-op hours, feel free to call our front desk directly at (555) 382-9100.

For the safety, security, and uninterrupted focus of our learners, all visitors must register for a scheduled tour or open house in advance. All adult campus guests must present valid government photo ID and receive a visitor badge upon arrival.

Visitor parking is located directly in front of the Main Commons in Lot A. Spaces clearly marked "Guest / Tour Visitor" are reserved for your arrival. Barrier-free ADA accessible parking with direct ramp access is situated adjacent to the main double doors.
